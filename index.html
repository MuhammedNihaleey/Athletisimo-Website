The method

Measured,
not guessed.

Every client follows the same four-step process, so the work is clear and the progress is visible.

See a real result
  1. 01

    Consult

    A free conversation on WhatsApp or a call about your goals, your training history, injuries and your schedule.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Baseline tests such as plank, push-up hold, boat pose and wall squat, plus body weight, so we know exactly where you are starting.

  3. 03

    Train

    A structured plan, online or in person, coached with attention to form and adjusted as your body adapts.

  4. 04

    Track

    Regular re-assessments summarised in a clear progress sheet, so you can see how far you've come.

Questions

Something else? Ask on WhatsApp →

How does online coaching work?

After a free consultation you get a plan built for your goals and the equipment you have. You log your sessions, send form videos when needed, and check in with me on WhatsApp. The plan is adjusted as you progress, wherever you are in the world.

Where do you train in person?

In Dubai and across the UAE: outdoors in parks, at your home, or in your building's gym. Message me your area and we'll find the setup that works for you.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Many programmes use bodyweight, a mat and minimal equipment. If you do have a gym, we'll make full use of it.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Every client starts with an assessment, so your programme begins where you are today, not where someone else is.

How is progress measured?

We repeat baseline fitness tests (holds, endurance and mobility) at regular intervals and track body weight where relevant. You receive a clear progress sheet showing your baseline, your latest result and the percentage change.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the format (online or in person) and how often we train. Send a message and I'll share the current packages that fit your goals.
